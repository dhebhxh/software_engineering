# Level 2 Information (暂定)

## 游戏地图
阶梯式地图，以平台形式搭建，四个密码机（暂定）随机出现在各处平台。

## 通关目标
玩家通过与四个密码机进行持续交互（读进度条）来完成密码破译，四个密码机均破译完毕后可以打开最终大门，实现通关。

## 主要玩法
在游玩过程中，地图中会随机生成怪物来攻击玩家，玩家需要通过录制分身操作，或让分身吸引怪物注意（分身可以与怪物产生碰撞，碰撞后分身和怪物均会消失），或让分身协助密码破译。在录制时分身的路线规划很大程度上会决定游戏进程，若怪物碰到玩家本体则游戏失败。

## 挑战
录制次数以及怪物出现次数都有一定上限。  
难易度（实际实现可只选一个，数字可调整）  
简单：录制次数*10 怪物刷新次数*3  
普通：录制次数*6  怪物刷新次数*3  
困难：录制次数*5  怪物刷新次数*5

## Game Map
A tiered map constructed in platform form, with four decryption terminals (tentative) randomly appearing across various platforms.

## Objective
Players must continuously interact with all four terminals (monitoring progress bars) to complete decryption. Once all terminals are decoded, the final door unlocks, achieving completion.

## Core Gameplay
During play, randomly spawned monsters will attack the player. Players must utilise recorded clones to navigate paths, distract monsters (clones can collide with monsters, causing both to vanish), or assist with decryption. Path planning for recorded clones significantly influences progress; contact between monsters and the player's main character results in failure.

## Challenges
Both recording attempts and monster spawns have capped limits.  
Difficulty Levels: (Implementation may select one only; values adjustable)

Normal: Recording attempts*6   Monster spawns*3  
Hard: Recording attempts*5   Monster spawns*5
