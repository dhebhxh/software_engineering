1. Over‑engineering Delayed the Delivery of Core Functionality
1.1 In the early design of multiple systems (such as the collision system and character control), the team focused excessively on future extensibility and tended to reserve interfaces for potential features. This caused the scope of requirements to expand continuously, increased design complexity, and prevented core functionality from being completed on time.
1.2 Solution: The team adopted an MVP‑first development strategy—prioritizing the implementation of the minimum viable functionality, ensuring clear logic and responsibility boundaries, and then extending features gradually. This effectively avoided unnecessary early‑stage complexity.
1.3 Lesson Learned: Clarifying priorities, adhering to MVP thinking, and maintaining cross‑module communication are essential for ensuring smooth project progress.

2. Designing Technical Solutions from Scratch Resulted in Low Efficiency
2.1 When designing technical solutions across multiple modules (such as collision‑detection strategies and input‑parsing logic), relying solely on self‑derived ideas required significant exploration time and led to low efficiency.
2.2 Solution: The team began actively referencing existing game engines, technical blogs, and open‑source projects to quickly understand common patterns and then tailor them to project needs. This significantly improved the efficiency of technical solution design.
2.3 Lesson Learned: The team established a more efficient technical learning approach: referencing mature solutions, extracting core logic, and adapting it to project requirements.

1 过度追求完美导致核心功能迟迟无法落地
1.1 在多个系统（如碰撞系统、角色控制等）的早期设计中，团队过度关注未来的扩展性，倾向于提前为潜在功能预留接口，导致需求边界不断扩大、设计复杂度上升，核心功能无法按时完成。
1.2 解决方式： 团队采用了 MVP 优先 的开发策略——优先实现最小可运行功能，确保逻辑与职责边界清晰，再逐步扩展功能。此举有效避免了前期不必要的复杂化。
1.3 经验教训：明确优先级、坚持 MVP 思维、以及保持跨模块沟通，是确保项目顺利推进的关键

2. 技术方案从零构思导致效率低下
2.1 在多个模块（例如碰撞检测策略、输入解析逻辑等）的技术方案设计中，如果完全依赖自我推导，往往需要投入大量时间探索，导致效率偏低。
2.2 解决方式： 团队开始主动参考已有游戏引擎、技术博客和开源项目，快速理解常见方案，并根据项目需求进行裁剪。这显著提升了技术方案设计的效率。
1.3 经验教训：团队总结出一套更高效的技术学习路径：参考成熟方案、提炼核心逻辑、并按项目需求裁剪落地。
